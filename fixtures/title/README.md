# title — Title painting (multicolor bitmap, smooth gradient + detail)

Ski troops and a horse-drawn sledge on a snow road at dusk, Svir front winter 1941–42; large smooth sky gradient over a detailed foreground.

## What it tests

The headline per-layer-dither demonstration: ordered dither on the sky, error diffusion on the foreground, a single warm accent (the burning farmhouse) that the solver must not lose. Background-colour search (16 candidates) matters here.
